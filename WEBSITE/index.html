Map Motion Studio sits at the intersection of geospatial storytelling and broadcast motion design. We turn raw data, terrain, and satellite imagery into sequences people actually want to watch — then bring the same craft to brand and title work.

Every project starts with the same question: what's the one thing this frame needs to say? Everything else gets cut.
